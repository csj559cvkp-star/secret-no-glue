For Andie

Distance has a strange way of making familiar things feel farther than they really are.

Home can start to feel like a place you have to miss instead of somewhere you can reach.

So I wanted to bring a little piece of it to you.

The voices, the memories, the people, the love — all gathered here, just to remind you that no matter how far away you are, you are never far from the hearts that know you best.

And today, I hope this feels a little like coming home.
